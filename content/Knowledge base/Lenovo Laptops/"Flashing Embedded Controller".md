The message "flashing embedded controlled - please do not power off" appears when booting the laptop.

This can sit for for a long time not doing anything with the power button not working either.

The only was to make this go away and to reboot the laptop is to use the small pinhole reset on the bottom of the laptop.

Using a paperclip or something as small, poke the hold until you feel a click. Hold there for 10 seconds. Once done, the laptop should reboot.
