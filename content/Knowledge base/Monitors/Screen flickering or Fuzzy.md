When the screen is unclear (e.g looks to be slightly blurry/vibrating), this could be a symptom of the monitor having the wrong refresh rate. This could especially be likely when it's only on one monitor (if the laptop is plugged into multiple.

- In display settings, choose the affected monitor and go to "advanced display settings near the bottom of the menu.
![[Pasted image 20241231131545.png]]
The refresh rate option will be there.

![[Pasted image 20241231131604.png]]

  
Change the option from the drop down and find which refresh rate stops the issue with the display.
