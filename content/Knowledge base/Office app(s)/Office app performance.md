If office apps are crashing, lagging or generally having issues opening, saving ect, theres a few things to try.

Only once all the basic steps have been tried (restart/hardware check) should you move on to these solutions.

- Check no plug-ins are affecting the apps. Disable all COM add-ins and try re-opening. Also try in safe mode

- Uninstalling and re-installing office
    - Can be done via add/remove programs then re-installed via the web at office 365.

- Re-naming the office folder in AppData
    - Ensure all office applications are closed and work has been saved.
    - Once folder has been re-named, the next office application to open will immediately create a new folder.
