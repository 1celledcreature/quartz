**Digital Forensics**
 
The purpose of Digital forensics is to investigate crimes and establish facts. This evolved from **computer forensics** after the wide spread use of digital systems e.g. computers + smartphones.
 
In defensive security, the focus of digital forensics is about analysing evidence of an attack, its perpetrators and other areas (such as intellectual property theft, cyber espionage and possession of unauthorised content.
