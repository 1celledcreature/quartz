**Threat Intelligence**
 
- Gather information about actual and potential enemies. A threat is any action that can disrupt or massively affect a system.
- Helps the company gather info to prepare against potential 'adversaries' (malware, virus' hackers ect)
- The purpose is to achieve 'threat-informed defence'
- Every company has different 'adversaries'.
    
    - Stealing customer data from phone companies.
    - Halting production of certain product (e.g production of petroleum)
- Example adversaries include:
    
    - Cyber army working for political reasons.
    - Ransomware group working for financial reasons
- Threat intelligence needs data, to collect, process and analyse.
- Data collection can be done from such places as:
    
    - Local sources
    - Network logs
    - Public sources (e.g online forums)
- The collection of data is done to format the information which is suitable for analysis. The analysis phase looks to gather info about the attackers and their motives. And to create a list of recommendations and actionable steps.
- Using threat intelligence, we look to learn about adversaries tactics, techniques and procedures. We can then identify them, predict their activity and hopefully then able to mitigate their attacks and prepare a response strategy.
